## The 5 Core Active Matter Character Class Builds

Creating tailored loadouts allows squads to adapt to unstable quantum shifts. Below are the five leading builds that define modern squad strategies.

### 1. The Heavy Gunner (Anchor / Breacher)
The Heavy Gunner serves as the squad's frontline anchor. Equipped with high-capacity suppression weapons like the PKM machine gun and backed by thick Titan armor plates, this build absorbs punishment while shredding anomalous creatures and pinning down enemy fireteams.

*   **Primary Weapon:** PKM Machine Gun (7.62x54mm) or SCAR-L (5.56mm)
*   **Secondary Weapon:** MB590 Shotgun or MP-443 Grach
*   **Armor Preference:** SF Helmet paired with Large Titan Armor Plates
*   **Tactical Gadget:** Incendiary Grenades & Streaming Injectors
*   **Playstyle:** Hold choke points inside science facilities and control high-value extraction zones.

### 2. The Recon Specialist (Scout / Spotter)
Information is lethal in quantum unstable zones. The Recon Specialist utilizes tactical aerial surveillance to pinpoint enemy ambush points and anomalous cluster spawns before your squad steps into danger.

*   **Primary Weapon:** M4A1 Assault Rifle with EXPS3 Collimator or TA31 4.0x Scope
*   **Secondary Weapon:** Scorpion EVO 3 or UMP45 SMG
*   **Armor Preference:** A3 Helmet with Lightweight Ceramic Armor Plates
*   **Tactical Gadget:** Scout Drone "Harpy" & Small Safe Containers
*   **Playstyle:** Deploy aerial drones from cover, ping hostile movement, and execute flanking maneuvers while enemies focus on your frontline teammates.

### 3. The Designated Marksman (Sniper / Overwatch)
Long-range precision eliminates threats before they enter active combat range. When building effective **Active Matter character classes** for solo or squad play, the Marksman provides critical overwatch across expansive outdoor maps.

*   **Primary Weapon:** M110A1 DMR (7.62x51mm) or SV-98 Bolt-Action Sniper Rifle
*   **Secondary Weapon:** MP5 Submachine Gun or M9 Pistol
*   **Armor Preference:** Tactical PASGT Helmet with Ceramic Armor Plates
*   **Optics:** 1P69 Scope 1-10x or Thermal Scope S350F 2.0x
*   **Playstyle:** Position on elevated terrain or industrial roofs to neutralize enemy snipers and lock down extraction extract points.

### 4. The CQB Infiltrator (Pointman / Rusher)
Built for speed and aggressive room clearing, the CQB Infiltrator thrives in close-quarters combat inside rusted Soviet laboratories and subterranean bunkers.

*   **Primary Weapon:** SPAS-12 Shotgun or SOK-94 Vepr Carbine
*   **Secondary Weapon:** UMP45 or MP5 Submachine Gun
*   **Armor Preference:** Tactical PASGT Helmet with Steel Plates (for high mobility and cost-effective defense)
*   **Melee Backup:** Brass Knuckles Knife "Bouncer" or War Hammer
*   **Playstyle:** Fast breach-and-clear tactics using F-1 fragmentation grenades to disorient opponents before eliminating them at point-blank range.

### 5. The Combat Engineer (Support / Logistics)
The backbone of extended raids, the Combat Engineer focuses on area defense, squad respawns, and deploying automated support assets to turn the tide during PvE boss encounters or multi-team skirmishes.

*   **Primary Weapon:** SCAR-L or SOK-94 Vepr Carbine
*   **Secondary Weapon:** Hunting Crossbow (silent takedowns)
*   **Armor Preference:** A3 Helmet with Medium/Large Titan Plates
*   **Tactical Tech:** Robot Dog "胡安一号" & Respawn Beacons
*   **Playstyle:** Deploy combat drones and defensive beacons to secure extraction zones while managing squad supply efficiency.

## Advanced Loadout Tips for Quantum Raids

To maximize raid success when evaluating all **Active Matter character classes**, apply these tactical practices:

1.  **Match Optics to Map Sightlines:** On large outdoor island maps with Soviet industrial structures, equip dual-optic systems like the 1P69 (1-10x) or TNG6 (1-6x). For claustrophobic laboratory interiors, switch to Moosemark or EXPS3 collimators.
2.  **Caliber Economy Matters:** If two squad members run 5.56mm ammo (such as M4A1 and SCAR-L), you will rapidly deplete squad ammunition reserves. Diversify weapon calibers across 7.62x54mm, 7.62x51mm, and 9x19mm.
3.  **Secure High-Tier Plates in Safe Containers:** Always carry backup Ceramic or Steel plates in your Medium Safe Container so you can hot-swap ruined armor after intense firefights without risking gear loss.
4.  **Leverage Thermal Sights Against Anomalies:** The Thermal Scope S350F cuts through anomalous environmental distortion and smoke, giving your Designated Marksman an undeniable tactical advantage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are there strictly locked Active Matter character classes?
No, Active Matter uses a flexible, gear-and-shell-based archetype system rather than permanently locked class trees. Players define their combat roles by selecting specialized character shells (like Shell "Reaper"), equipping designated weapon categories, installing specific armor plate materials, and manufacturing advanced tactical recipes via the Replicator.

### What is the best class build for solo players in Active Matter?
For solo raids, the Recon Specialist build is widely considered optimal by player experience. Utilizing lightweight armor, silenced carbines (like the M4A1), and the Scout Drone "Harpy" allows solo operators to scout hostile squads, avoid deadly anomalies, and extract quietly without triggering high-risk firefights.

### How do armor plate tiers impact class mobility?
Armor plates directly affect operative speed, stamina consumption, and recoil recovery. Large Titan plates provide top-tier ballistic protection for frontline Heavy Gunners but reduce movement speed. In contrast, small Ceramic and Steel plates offer higher mobility for Scouts and CQB Infiltrators at the cost of sustained multi-hit durability.

### What role does the Replicator play in defining Active Matter character classes?
The Replicator allows players to manufacture specialized gear blueprints, including combat companions (such as the Robot Dog "胡安一号"), aerial reconnaissance drones ("Harpy"), and mobility vehicles ("Bonecrusher"). Access to these recipes allows players to transform standard combat loadouts into high-utility Support, Recon, or Heavy tactical classes.
