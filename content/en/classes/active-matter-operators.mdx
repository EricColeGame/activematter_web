The core combat workhorse in *Active Matter*. Armed with the hard-hitting SCAR-L or M4A1 and supported by incendiary grenades, the Breacher clears anomalous nests and ambushes rival extractors at medium-to-close quarters.

### 3. The Heavy Juggernaut
When contesting active matter harvesting nodes, the Heavy Juggernaut provides unmatched sustained firepower. Anchored by the **PKM machine gun** and protected by Titan armor plates, this build locks down extraction corridors against multiple enemy squads.

### 4. The Phantom Marksman
Equipped with variable-zoom optics like the **1P69 Scope (1-10x)** or the **Thermal Scope S350F**, the Marksman eliminates threats before they enter rifle range. This role is essential on open island maps containing vast industrial complexes.

### 1. Manage Quantum Unstable Zones
Quantum anomalies warp physics, audio cues, and bullet trajectories across the map. When entering scientific research labs, keep your **Streaming Injectors** and painkillers bound to quick-access slots to counteract sudden temporal bleed.

### 2. Prioritize Safe Container Storage
Always store high-value items—such as **Active Matter Crystals**, rare scope attachments (like the Thermal S350F), and replicator blueprints—inside your **Small or Medium Safe Containers**. Items locked inside safe containers stay with your operator even if you fail to extract.

### 3. Deploy Multi-Angle Overwatches
When harvesting a passive source of active matter, never bunch up. Station your Marksman 150–200 meters away with an **M110A1** or **SV-98**, while the Heavy Anchor locks down the immediate extraction terminal with a **PKM**.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Active Matter Operators

### Can Active Matter operators switch shells and loadouts between raids?
Yes. Operators can freely switch their active character shell (such as Shell "Reaper"), modify weapon platforms with various scopes and collimators, and reconfigure armor plate setups in their hideout stash between every raid deployment.

### What is the most effective solo build for Active Matter operators?
For solo players, combining the **Scorpion EVO 3** or **M4A1** with lightweight **Ceramic Armor Plates** and the **Scout Drone "Harpy"** offers the best balance. This configuration provides the speed needed to outmaneuver squads and the scouting capabilities to avoid fatal ambushes.

### What happens to an operator's gear upon death in a raid?
If you are eliminated during an extraction raid, any unprotected weapons, armor plates, backpacks, and loose loot are left behind for other players to scavenge. However, items stored inside your **Small or Medium Safe Container** remain securely in your inventory after returning to the main menu.

### How do Respawn Beacons function for squad-based operators?
Respawn Beacons allow fallen squad members to rejoin the active raid. When an operator places a beacon in a safe, concealed spot, a defeated teammate can deploy back into the match, provided the beacon remains undiscovered and undamaged by hostile forces.
