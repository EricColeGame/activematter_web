## Core Gameplay: Time Loops, Raids, and Quantum Extraction

*Active Matter* blends hyper-realistic weapon handling with cosmic SCP-style surrealism. In the game's narrative, you take on the role of an operative trapped inside a continuous time loop. Each death resets your physical timeline, resurrecting you at your base while preserving your hard-won syndicate knowledge.

Raids drop operatives into "quantum unstable zones." These sectors are rich in active matter—a volatile, reality-warping substance that alters gravity, distorts time, and mutates living organisms. Your primary objective is to harvest this matter from clusters, defeat transformed creatures, eliminate rival operators, and extract before the zone collapses out of space-time entirely.

```
[Deploy via Quantum Drop] ➔ [Scout Unstable Zones] ➔ [Harvest Active Matter & Tech] 
       ➔ [Engage Mutants / Rival Operators] ➔ [Secure Extraction Zone Before Collapse]
```

## Day-One Tactics and Syndicate Progression

Surviving quantum raids requires a calculated approach to inventory management and risk assessment. Formulating a coherent syndicate progression plan ahead of the **Active Matter launch date** gives players an immediate competitive advantage over less prepared squads.

### 1. Balance Loot Value Against Extraction Proximity
Active matter is heavy and volatile. Carrying excessive quantities slows operator mobility and increases your signature on rival scanners. Prioritize extracting smaller, stable loads during your early raids to secure baseline hideout upgrades before risking deep laboratory dives.

### 2. Treat Anomalies as Tactical Cover
Physics-bending anomalies are lethal if touched blindly, but skilled operatives can use them defensively. Luring aggressive mutant creatures or overconfident enemy players into gravitational distortion fields can win an engagement without firing a single round.

### 3. Coordinate Syndicate Specialization
When deploying as a trio or quad, assign specific team roles:
- **Point Scout:** Carries anomaly detectors and light submachine guns.
- **Support Gunner:** Controls corridors with suppressive fire and carries surplus ammo.
- **Harvester:** Equips high-capacity extraction containers to secure active matter quickly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the confirmed Active Matter launch date?
The official **Active Matter launch date** is September 15, 2026. The game will unlock globally on Steam for PC and compatible Linux/SteamOS systems.

### Does Active Matter feature solo matchmaking?
Yes. Players can deploy into quantum unstable zones completely solo or team up in small squads. In all modes, solo operators face both environmental anomalies, mutated creatures, and other players in a shared PvPvE environment.

### Why is an SSD strictly required for Active Matter?
The game relies on dynamic asset streaming to handle sudden dimensional shifts, physics anomalies, and fast-paced extraction transitions. Traditional hard disk drives (HDDs) cause severe hitching and loading delays during dimensional collapses.

### Is Active Matter a pure PvP game?
*Active Matter* is a PvPvE extraction shooter. While rival players represent the most lethal threat in any raid, operators must also contend with SCP-inspired monsters, hostile anomalies, and time-loop decay mechanics.
