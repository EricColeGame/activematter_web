## What Is Active Matter? Multiverse Extraction and PvPvE Chaos

At its core, *Active Matter* plunges operatives into quantum unstable zones—isolated pockets where conventional laws of physics and temporal boundaries have completely unraveled. Players enter these fractured realities on dangerous salvage raids, competing against rival operators while battling hostile anomalies and regional threats.

The primary objective revolves around harvesting **Active Matter**, an enigmatic, volatile substance capable of rewriting physical properties and fueling futuristic manufacturing technologies. Extraction points are dynamic, firefights erupt across shifting terrain, and one misstep against an anomalous rift can erase an entire haul in seconds.

### Surviving Anomalous Field Conditions

When exploring these ruins during an **Active Matter playtest** drop, keep these environmental quirks in mind:

*   **Temporal Shifts:** Certain corridors and laboratory vaults flicker between time states. A doorway open in one moment may seal shut behind a temporal barrier seconds later.
*   **Passive Matter Emitters:** These glowing crystalline formations continuously leak active matter. Securing them triggers automated regional alerts, drawing both hostile patrols and opportunistic player squads.
*   **Sound Propagation in Industrial Concrete:** Rust-eaten sheet metal and wet concrete echo footsteps heavily. Swapping bulky steel plates for lighter ceramic armor helps keep your approach quiet during CQB interior sweeps.

### Medical and Stimulant Management

Healing damage requires a targeted approach rather than a universal health kit:

*   **Standard First Aid Kits:** Patch light bleeds and restore baseline limb health over a short duration.
*   **Big First Aid Kits:** Accelerate trauma recovery and repair heavily damaged torsos.
*   **Painkillers:** Eliminate movement debuffs caused by fractured legs, allowing you to sprint toward extraction under fire.
*   **Streaming Injectors:** High-tech stimulants that temporarily boost stamina recovery and accelerate active matter absorption rates during node extraction.

## 5 Pro Tips for Your Next Active Matter Playtest Session

Navigating your first **Active Matter playtest** session requires patience, resource conservation, and situational awareness. Follow these field-tested tactics:

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                       RAID SURVIVAL CHECKLIST                                 |
|                                                                               |
|  [1] Place Active Matter Crystals directly into Safe Containers immediately.  |
|  [2] Deploy the Harpy Drone before breaching major Soviet research bunkers.  |
|  [3] Set a Respawn Beacon in a hidden exterior bush prior to node extraction. |
|  [4] Pack at least 2 spare Armor Plates per engagement sector.                |
|  [5] Avoid sprinting across open temporal rifts while carrying full bags.     |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

1.  **Prioritize Secure Storage for Crystals:** Raw Active Matter crystals are the lifeblood of account progression. The moment you harvest a crystal cluster, transfer it into your Small or Medium Safe Container so you keep the yield even if eliminated.
2.  **Synchronize Beacon Placements:** If you enter as a fireteam, coordinate where you drop your **Beacon**. Conceal it inside foliage or neglected maintenance shafts outside major combat zones to ensure safe team redeployments.
3.  **Leverage Thermal Optics on High Ground:** Scopes like the **Thermal Scope S350F 2.0x** expose snipers concealed in dense foliage or shadowy industrial catwalks. Pair thermal sights with high-velocity platforms like the M110A1 or SV-98.
4.  **Use Drones to Bait Ambushes:** Send your Scout Drone "Harpy" into blind rooms first. Player reports indicate that enemy squads frequently fire upon incoming drones, instantly revealing their defensive firing lanes.
5.  **Monitor Ammo Calibers Closely:** The game uses realistic ammunition classifications (e.g., 7.62x39mm, 7.62x51mm, 7.62x54mm, 5.56mm, 9x19mm, .45 ACP, and 12 Gauge). Double-check your magazine types before deploying to avoid loading incompatible rounds into modified carbines.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I join an Active Matter playtest?
Access to the **Active Matter playtest** is primarily granted through official store pre-orders, developer alpha/beta invitations, and dedicated testing waves announced on the studio's official community channels. Registering your account on the official portal ensures you are notified when new testing servers go live.

### Does progress carry over from an Active Matter playtest to the full launch?
In most testing phases, player stash inventories, gathered credits, and unlocked replicator blueprints undergo periodic server wipes to test economy balancing. However, edition-specific rewards—such as exclusive character Shells, veteran dog tags, and base pre-order weapon bundles—will be credited back to your account for subsequent test cycles and the commercial launch.

### Can I play Active Matter as a solo operator?
Yes. While squads of two or three operators have greater fire support, solo players can utilize stealth, autonomous combat companions (like the Robot Dog "胡安一号"), and cautious extraction pathing to outmaneuver larger teams. Solo operators also benefit from keeping all harvested Active Matter to themselves.

### What happens if I die during an extraction raid?
If your operative is eliminated and you have no active respawn beacon deployed, you lose all unprotected gear, weapons, and backpack loot. Items stashed inside your Safe Container remain intact and return to your main shelter stash upon returning to the lobby.
