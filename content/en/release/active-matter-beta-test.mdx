## Raid Mechanics: Exploring the Multiverse Anomalies

Raids in the **Active Matter beta test** revolve around unstable quantum zones located outside standard space and time. Players deploy either solo or in small squads to harvest raw active matter from passive node clusters and derelict facilities while battling hostile creatures and rival PMC operators.

The primary environment featured in testing is a massive island complex dominated by weathered Soviet industrial compounds, deep subterranean rail tunnels, and secretive scientific laboratories.

### Quantum Raid Phases and Flow

1.  **Insertion & Calibration:** Operators spawn on the periphery of the island. Initial minutes must be spent securing perimeter loot, scouting nearby anomalous rifts, and clearing initial wandering threats.
2.  **Matter Siphon Contestation:** Central map locations activate passive matter emitters. Rival squads converge on these locations, creating intense PvP firefights where thermal optics and smoke grenades prove decisive.
3.  **Space-Time Distortion:** As the raid timer winds down, the quantum zone destabilizes. Safe zones contract, forcing remaining players toward active rift extract points.
4.  **Extraction or Collapse:** Operators must reach an extraction beacon, hold off final waves of defenders or ambushing players, and extract before the instance collapses entirely.

[Infiltration] ---> [Scout Labs & Siphons] ---> [PvPvE Hotzone Clash] ---> [Rift Extraction]

## 5 Essential Tips for Your First Active Matter Beta Test Raids

*   **Deploy Respawn Beacons Strategically:** Do not hold beacons in your backpack indefinitely. Deploy them in concealed, covered locations near high-value lab entrances to give your squad a second chance during heated fire-fights.
*   **Secure Crystals in Safe Containers:** Active Matter Crystals and rare lab keys should immediately go into your small or medium safe container to protect progression even if your squad wipes.
*   **Balance Armor Weight:** Equipping heavy steel plates provides high durability but slows your movement and sprint stamina. For fast loot runs, lighter ceramic plates offer a tactical advantage.
*   **Listen to Environmental Anomalies:** Unstable rift locations emit distinct audio cues. If space-time distortion sounds intensify, you are either near a high-tier loot cluster or an imminent temporal collapse.
*   **Leverage Drones for Scouting:** Before storming Soviet research bunkers, deploy the Harpy drone to tag defensive angles and check for camping players holding high-ground corridors.

You can gain direct access to the **Active Matter beta test** by pre-ordering any digital edition through the official game store. Tiers range from the Standard Edition up to the Elite Squad Edition, each granting immediate access keys and starter item packages.

Based on player experience from extraction test phases, progression wipes are standard practice before full public release. Yes, players can upgrade their digital package through the official store to unlock additional stash expansions, advanced Replicator blueprints like the Harpy drone or Bonecrusher quad, and bonus Standard Edition gift keys for squadmates.

The beta features sprawling anomalous zones centered around a massive island containing decaying Soviet industrial facilities, classified underground research bunkers, and quantum rift anomalies that distort gravity and line of sight.
