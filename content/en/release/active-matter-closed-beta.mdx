## What Is Active Matter? The Multiverse Extraction Shooter Explained

*Active Matter* blends high-stakes tactical shooter fundamentals with unpredictable quantum anomalies. Players step into the boots of an operative trapped in a fractured loop across parallel realities. In every raid, you must deploy into high-risk anomaly zones, harvest valuable Active Matter substances, engage rival scavengers, neutralize mutated entities, and reach an extraction point before the localized spacetime collapse wipes out your squad.

The development team, Matter Team, features creative and technical talent with deep roots in games like *Enlisted* and *CRSED: Cuisine Royale*. This pedigree ensures that while the sci-fi setting introduces bizarre, reality-warping phenomena, weapon handling, ballistics, and tactical movement remain grounded in believable physics.

| Core Feature | Description | Tactical Impact |

| **Active Matter Harvesting** | Extracting exotic, shifting matter from anomaly clusters. | Generates valuable currency and crafting materials, but exposes your position. |

According to early community reports and developer insights, the core loop is designed around risk mitigation. Carrying more Active Matter increases your character's value, but it can also distort your immediate surroundings, drawing attention from both rival operators and multiversal horrors.

## Survival Strategies: Mastering the Anomaly Zones

Surviving raids during the **Active Matter closed beta** requires unlearning standard tactical habits. In ordinary extraction shooters, high ground offers safety and superior sightlines; in *Active Matter*, sudden spatial distortions can turn high ground into an exposed, gravity-inverted kill zone. 

To help you extract with your loot intact, use this tactical reference matrix when assessing anomalies and encounters in the field:

| Threat / Anomaly Type | Visual & Audio Indicators | Danger Level | Recommended Countermeasure |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Quantum Rift** | Flickering air, low-frequency hum, visual tearing. | Medium | Avoid sprinting through; toss throwable objects first to check stability. |
| **Active Matter Siphon** | Glowing purple/blue geysers, distorted gravity fields. | High | Clear the perimeter of AI guards and human snipers before harvesting. |
| **Mutated Husk Entity** | Erratic movement, guttural vocalizations, immune to flashbangs. | Medium | Aim for central kinetic cores; use armor-piercing ammunition to conserve stamina. |
| **Rival Extraction Campers** | Suppressed gunfire near exfil beacon, lingering smoke. | Extreme | Approach extract points from off-angles; utilize recon drones or radar sweeps. |
| **Reality Collapse Wave** | Red skies, increasing screen static, seismic tremors. | Lethal | Disengage from combat immediately and sprint to the nearest stable extraction point. |

Always manage your inventory weight carefully. Heavy armor vests and heavily modified assault rifles absorb incoming damage, but reduced mobility can prove fatal when an anomaly zone begins collapsing around you.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How will I know if I received access to the Active Matter closed beta?
Invitations are dispatched directly to the email address tied to your registered Gaijin account. The email will provide complete instructions on how to access the private client download, redeem your access key, and join the private testing Discord channel.

### Is the Active Matter closed beta available on PS5 or Xbox Series X/S?
No. The closed beta is currently exclusive to PC via the Gaijin launcher. Console versions are planned for future development phases, but initial technical testing and performance tuning are focused strictly on Windows platforms.

### Will my character progression carry over from the beta to the full game?
Gameplay progression, including gathered gear, harvested materials, and character stats, will be wiped periodically between test phases to maintain testing balance. However, commemorative gifts, beta tester cosmetics, and profile badges will be permanently saved to your account and accessible in the full release.

### Do I need to buy a founder's pack to play the closed beta?
Currently, registration for the **Active Matter closed beta** is free via the official website. While guaranteed access packs may be introduced in future testing phases, initial testing pools are selected directly from the pool of registered Gaijin account applicants.
