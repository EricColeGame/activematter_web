## Tactical Preparation and Loadout Strategies

To hit the ground running once servers open, players must optimize their gear management. Community reports from early alpha sessions highlight several critical loadout strategies for beginner and veteran extraction shooters alike.

```

│ POINTMAN: M4A1 + EXPS3, Ceramic Armor, Flashlight      │

└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Balance Armor Weight and Mobility:** Titan plates offer immense protection against rifle calibers like 7.62x51mm, but the added weight degrades stamina recovery during extraction sprints.
2. **Prioritize Replicator Queue Management:** Players with expanded queues can continuously manufacture medical supplies, painkiller injectors, and ammunition boxes between raids.
3. **Deploy Scout Drones Early:** Using the "Harpy" drone to scout dense Soviet laboratory courtyards reduces the risk of ambushes by opposing fireteams.
4. **Guard Active Matter Nodes:** Nodes produce passive crystal generation but attract AI guards and opportunistic players. Set perimeter crossfires before extracting node cores.
