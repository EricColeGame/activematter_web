### 1. M4A1 — The Ultimate All-Rounder Assault Rifle
The M4A1 is widely considered the gold standard for assault rifles in the game. With a 750 RPM firing rate and a low 0.12 recoil rating, it allows players to hold tight groupings during medium-range sprays. Its lightweight 2.70 kg frame ensures rapid aim-down-sights (ADS) transition, while the 2.67-second reload keeps downtime minimal during prolonged firefights.

### 2. SCAR-L — Precision and Stability
The SCAR-L trades a bit of raw fire rate (625 RPM) for laser-like stability. With an ultra-low recoil rating of 0.10 and a swift 2.30-second reload, it excels at mid-to-long-range tap firing. Player experience confirms that its steady cadence makes landing consecutive upper-torso and headshots significantly easier than with higher-recoil alternatives.

### 3. AK-103 — Heavy Caliber Stopping Power
For operatives who favor raw kinetic punch, the AK-103 chambers the hard-hitting 7.62×39mm round. While its 0.14 recoil requires practiced pull-down control, its per-bullet damage shreds through medium body armor. At 600 RPM, it functions as a devastating burst rifle in disciplined hands.

### 4. MP5 & Scorpion EVO 3 — Close-Quarters Dominators
Submachine guns excel during building breaches and indoor extractions:
* **MP5:** Boasts a minimal 0.07 recoil stat. It delivers surgical accuracy during hipfire maneuvers, making it perfect for aggressive pushers.
* **Scorpion EVO 3:** Unleashes a blistering 1080 RPM. It boasts the fastest theoretical TTK in close-quarters combat, melting unarmored and armored targets before they can return fire.

### 5. SV-98 — Long-Range Precision
The SV-98 is the premier bolt-action sniper rifle. Chambered in high-velocity 7.62×51mm, it delivers clean, single-shot eliminations across open fields. A surprisingly fast 2.20-second chamber reload keeps your crosshairs active for follow-up shots on moving squads.

### 6. MB590 & SPAS-12 — Defensive Shotguns
* **MB590:** A pump-action powerhouse that reliably delivers one-shot kills in close-quarters combat. At 3.30 kg, it remains mobile enough for aggressive building sweeps.
* **SPAS-12:** Offers rapid semi-automatic follow-up shots. While its 7.86-second full reload is punishing, its burst potential makes it unmatched when locking down narrow corridors.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are the overall Active Matter best weapons for solo players?
For solo operatives, the **M4A1** and **MP5** are the best choices. The M4A1 handles medium-to-long-range engagements reliably, while the MP5 allows you to win close-quarters surprise encounters with minimal recoil and rapid hipfire agility.

### Which Active Matter best weapons are easiest for beginners to control?
The **MP5** submachine gun and the **SCAR-L** assault rifle are the easiest guns to control. The MP5 features an exceptionally low 0.07 recoil value, while the SCAR-L's steady 625 RPM fire rate prevents your crosshairs from climbing uncontrollably during sustained fire.

### Is the AK-103 better than the M4A1 in Active Matter?
The AK-103 deals more damage per shot due to its 7.62×39mm caliber, making it superior for experienced players who can manage its 0.14 recoil. However, the M4A1 remains the more versatile all-around rifle due to its faster 750 RPM rate of fire, lighter weight, and easier recoil pattern.

### Why is the SPAS-12 ranked lower in reload speed compared to other shotguns?
While the SPAS-12 offers devastating semi-automatic burst fire, its individual shell-loading mechanic results in an extensive 7.86-second full reload time. Community reports highlight that running dry with a SPAS-12 in a multi-enemy firefight is extremely dangerous, making the MB590 a safer pick for general play.
