## Frequently Asked Questions About Active Matter Weapons

### How often does the Active Matter weapons list change with balance updates?
Developers frequently adjust firearm balance, recoil patterns, and armor penetration curves during major content patches and seasonal resets. When new anomalous zones open, experimental prototypes and balance passes routinely shift which guns sit at the top of the competitive tier list.

### Which starter firearm from the Active Matter weapons list should new operatives prioritize?
New operatives should prioritize the **Markov-74** or the **Kestrel Carbine**. Both rifles utilize widely accessible ammunition (5.45×39mm and 5.56×45mm), feature manageable baseline recoil, and can be maintained using common weapon repair kits found in low-threat sectors.

### How does armor penetration work in Active Matter?
Every bullet caliber has an assigned Penetration Class rating ranging from 1 to 6. If your bullet's penetration value is equal to or higher than the target's armor tier, it deals full base damage directly to the operative's health pool. If the penetration value is lower, the armor absorbs the kinetic impact, taking durability damage while significantly mitigating the flesh damage received.

### Can anomalous prototype weapons be repaired with standard kits?
No. Prototype and matter-infused weapons require refined anomaly components and specialized workshop benches to repair and maintain. If an experimental weapon drops to zero durability in the field, its energetic systems will destabilize, rendering it unusable until processed at your base facility.
