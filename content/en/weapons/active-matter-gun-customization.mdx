## Getting Started: Safehouse Workbench vs. Quick-Mod System

In *Active Matter*, weapon modification takes place within two primary interfaces: the full safehouse workbench (or Weapon Wall) and the quick-mod gear interface within your character inventory. Understanding when and how to use each system saves vital preparation time between raids.

```
Inventory / Weapon Wall (I) ➔ Select Weapon ➔ Right-Click Context Menu ➔ "Modding" ➔ Select Component Slot ➔ Preview & Install
```

The dedicated workbench gives you an unobstructed, modular view of your firearm. Here, you can inspect each individual attachment point, rotate the 3D model, and review the real-time stat impacts on recoil, weight, Aim Down Sight (ADS) speed, and effective range.

### Step-by-Step Workbench Setup

To build or modify a weapon from scratch in your safehouse:

1. Open your inventory screen using the **I** key or walk up to the Weapon Wall in your safehouse.
2. Highlight and right-click the firearm you wish to tune to open the contextual action menu.
3. Select **Modding** to enter the dedicated 3D inspection workbench.
4. Click on an available attachment node (such as the muzzle, optics rail, magazine well, or buffer tube).
5. Highlight an owned attachment to preview its exact stat alterations, then click to confirm installation.

### Using the Quick-Mod Feature

When you need to adjust your kit rapidly before stepping into a match queue, the quick-mod system provides an automated shortcut. On any equipped weapon card in your inventory screen, click the small gear icon. 

This shortcut opens a streamlined overlay where you can toggle "auto-mod" for individual component slots. The auto-mod system automatically equips the highest-tier compatible parts residing in your stash, bypassing the need to navigate through nested workbench menus.

### Modding Interface Comparison

| Feature | Safehouse Workbench | Quick-Mod System |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Access Location** | Safehouse / Main Menu Weapon Wall | Inventory Screen (Gear Icon on Item Card) |
| **Stat Preview** | Full 3D inspection with granular stat deltas | Abbreviated compatibility list |
| **Automation** | Manual attachment slotting | One-click auto-equip for available stash parts |
| **Best Used For** | Detailed loadout tuning and stat balancing | Rapid kit assembly right before raid departure |
| **Mid-Raid Availability** | Disabled during active matches | Limited quick-swapping via field inventory |

## Attachment Slots and Weapon Compatibility

When approaching **Active Matter gun customization** for the first time, recognizing how different weapon platforms accept attachments is crucial. Firearms do not share universal attachment rails; an optic mount designed for a modern western carbine will not mount onto a vintage surplus receiver without an adapter rail.

Every weapon features distinct mounting points across key areas of its chassis. Optimizing your firearm requires balancing the benefits of each attachment against added weight and bulk.

### Core Modification Slots

* **Optics & Sights:** Ranges from unmagnified reflex sights and holographic collimators for rapid close-quarters target acquisition to high-magnification variable scopes for long-range reconnaissance.
* **Barrels & Muzzles:** Houses muzzle brakes for vertical kick reduction, compensators for horizontal drift correction, and suppressors for acoustic concealment and muzzle flash elimination.
* **Stocks & Cheek Rests:** Balances overall weapon stability, felt recoil, and shoulder transition speeds. Adjustable stocks allow you to tune weapon length to offset heavy front-end attachments.
* **Magazines:** Encompasses standard-capacity box magazines, lightweight polymer variants that enhance reload speeds, and high-capacity drum magazines that trade ergonomics for sustained firepower.
* **Handguards & Underbarrel Mounts:** Accommodates vertical foregrips, angled tactical grips, flashlights, and infrared laser pointers that improve point-fire accuracy.

### Attachment Slot Breakdown and Tactical Trade-offs

| Slot Category | Common Modifications | Primary Stat Boost | Primary Drawback / Trade-off |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Optics** | Collimators, Red Dots, 4x Prismatic Scopes | Target acquisition clarity, zoom level | Slight increase to ADS transition time |
| **Muzzle Devices** | Compensators, Muzzle Brakes, Suppressors | Recoil dampening, reduced acoustic profile | Increases overall weapon length and weight |
| **Stocks** | Skeletonized Stocks, Fixed Stocks, Cheek Risers | Recoil recovery rate, stability while aiming | Slower movement speed while aiming |
| **Magazines** | Quick-draw Mag Pockets, Extended Drums | Sustained fire volume, faster tactical reloads | Slower reload animation, severe ADS penalties (Drums) |
| **Underbarrel** | Angled Grips, Vertical Grips, Handstops | Horizontal recoil stabilization, point-fire spread | Slight penalty to unholstering speed |

## How to Acquire Weapon Mods: Shop vs. In-Raid Scavenging

Securing the components required for advanced builds involves a mix of merchant trading and high-risk field scavenging. While exploring anomalous hot zones yields rare, high-tier military attachments, relying solely on extraction RNG can leave your primary weapons underperforming.

### Purchasing Through the Safehouse Shop

The most consistent method to upgrade your arsenal is through the integrated safehouse market. You can view all available hardware via the official [Gaijin Entertainment store and platform services](https://store.gaijin.net/) or access the in-game merchant directly through your dashboard.

To browse the full catalog:
1. Open your main menu (**ESC** or **I**).
2. Click the **Shop** tab located in the upper navigation bar.
3. Select the **Weapon Mods** subcategory to view optics, muzzle devices, stocks, and magazines.

> **Trader Stock Tip:** Shop inventory rotates periodically. High-tier military attachments and experimental suppression devices often unlock as you complete faction contracts and raise your standing with safehouse traders.

### The Fast-Filter Buying Shortcut

Instead of manually scrolling through hundreds of parts in the general shop, use the context-sensitive purchase feature:

1. Open your stash inventory.
2. Right-click the specific firearm you want to build.
3. Select **"Buy Ammunition and Mods"**.

This immediately filters the merchant's active stock to display **only** the attachments, magazines, and ammunition calibers guaranteed to fit that specific firearm. This eliminates accidental purchases of incompatible mounting rings, dovetail brackets, or incorrect calibers.

## Optimizing Weapon Stats: ADS Speed, Recoil, and Ergonomics

Understanding the specific slots within **Active Matter gun customization** helps players prioritize their budget and build balanced loadouts rather than stacking random high-tier parts. Every attachment carries positive and negative stat multipliers that dramatically alter firearm handling.

Over-modding a weapon with heavy drum magazines, reinforced steel stocks, and bulky suppressors will turn it into a heavy, sluggish platform. In close-quarters battles, having a slow ADS speed means your opponent can raise their weapon and fire before you even align your sight reticle.

### Finding the Sweet Spot: ADS vs. Recoil Control

When assembling a firearm, identify the weapon's intended combat role before choosing parts:

* **Close-Quarters Combat (CQC):** Focus on ergonomics and ADS speed. Use lightweight skeleton stocks, compact micro-reflex sights, and short polymer magazines.
* **Medium-to-Long Range Engagements:** Prioritize first-shot recoil recovery, horizontal sway dampening, and optical clarity. Heavy barrels, compensators, and magnified scopes excel here.

Always take freshly customized firearms to the safehouse firing range before deploying. Fire full-auto bursts to monitor muzzle climb patterns and practice snapping between targets at varying distances to verify that your ADS transition speed feels responsive.

### Tactical Build Archetypes

| Build Archetype | Primary Stat Focus | Key Attachment Priorities | Ideal Combat Range |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Point-Man CQB** | Max Ergonomics & Rapid ADS | Micro Red Dot, Shortened Handguard, Ergonomic Pistol Grip | 0 – 25 Meters |
| **Balanced Patrol** | Moderate Recoil Control & Clear Optics | 1x/4x Hybrid Holographic, Compensator, Angled Foregrip | 20 – 75 Meters |
| **Designated Marksman** | Maximum Stability & Range Velocity | 4x–8x Precision Scope, Extended Heavy Barrel, Fixed Stock | 60 – 150+ Meters |
| **Infiltration / Stealth** | Acoustic & Visual Signature Reduction | Threaded QD Suppressor, Subsonic Ammo Mags, Laser Module | Any (Flanking Engagements) |

## Managing Risk: Extraction Economy and Weapon Loss

Smart resource allocation is the backbone of efficient **Active Matter gun customization**. Because *Active Matter* is an extraction shooter, any firearm you carry into an anomaly zone is at risk of being lost if you are eliminated.

Community reports and player experiences suggest that over-investing in budget weapon frames is one of the quickest ways to drain your safehouse credits early on. If you attach a high-end suppressor and a rare variable optic to an unranked surplus receiver, you risk losing expensive parts without gaining the base ballistic benefits of a higher-tier firearm.

### Safehouse Rules for Weapon Modifications

* **Permanent Attachment:** Mods stay mounted to your weapon permanently in your stash until you manually detach them in the workbench.
* **Extraction Security:** Extracting safely from a raid guarantees that your weapon, along with every attached modification, returns to your safehouse intact.
* **Death Penalties:** If you perish inside a raid zone, any weapon carried on your operative—including all attached optics, grips, and muzzle devices—drops with your corpse for rivals to loot.

### Stash Budgeting and Modding Tiers

| **Standard Operative** | Moderate Investment | Mid-Tier Assault Rifles | Compensator, Ergonomic Grip, 1x/2x Optic | Team patrols, general contract hunting |

## Active Matter Gun Customization FAQ

### How do I open the weapon modding menu in Active Matter?
You can access the modding menu by opening your inventory (**I** key) or visiting the Weapon Wall in your safehouse. Right-click any firearm in your stash and select **Modding** to enter the full 3D workbench. Alternatively, click the small gear icon on an equipped weapon's item card for the quick-mod interface.

### What is the easiest way to buy compatible mods for a specific gun?
The fastest way to buy matching parts is through the context-sensitive store filter. Right-click the firearm in your inventory and choose **"Buy Ammunition and Mods"**. The in-game market will automatically filter its catalog to display only attachments, magazines, and ammo types compatible with that weapon platform.

### Does Active Matter gun customization allow for part swapping mid-raid?
You cannot open the full safehouse 3D workbench while actively deployed inside a raid zone. However, community reports confirm you can perform basic field modifications—such as attaching unmagnified optics, hot-swapping compatible magazines, or utilizing the quick-mod system—directly through your character inventory screen during quiet moments in a match.

### What happens to my custom weapon mods if I die during an extraction run?
If you are eliminated during a raid, your equipped firearm and all mounted attachments drop in the field and can be looted by rival players or scavengers. To retain your customized build, you must successfully reach an extraction beacon and leave the zone alive.
