## Mounting Systems and Rail Types in Active Matter

Before installing any upgrades, understanding how **Active Matter weapon attachments** connect to your firearm receiver is essential. Attachments do not use a universal slot; they require specific rails or barrel interfaces. Equipping an incompatible part in the field will leave you vulnerable.

According to player experience and community reports, weapons in *Active Matter* generally adhere to four primary mounting standards:

- **Short Picatinny Rail:** Standard tactical rail found on modern Western carbines, SMGs, and adapted Russian platforms. Supports compact collimators, low-magnification scopes, and tactical accessories.
- **Long Picatinny Rail:** Extended top rail standard on heavy marksman platforms, sniper rifles, and full-length combat rifles. Required for high-power scopes and thermal imaging systems.
- **Dovetail High Mount:** Side-rail bracket standard on traditional Soviet/Russian weapon platforms such as the AK-103 and Vepr-KM VPO.
- **Dedicated Stock and Muzzle Mounts:** Specialized receiver interfaces (Standard AR buffer tubes, AK tang mounts, Shotgun mounts, and threaded barrels categorized by caliber).

Always verify your receiver's rail type in the armory before heading into an active extraction zone.

Every weapon mod in *Active Matter* occupies two critical resource pools: **Weight (kg)** and **Inventory Volume**.

```
|  => Total: 0.37 kg | 45 Volume (High Mobility & Free Space) |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
```

Community reports highlight several practical guidelines for managing gear bulk:

1. **Do Not Over-Equip Secondary Weapons:** If you carry a backup SMG, use lightweight mods like the *VZ1* and *Mini 556*. Save heavy attachments for your primary combat rifle.
2. **Account for Looting Volume:** Extracting valuable barter items requires empty backpack grid volume. Carrying bulky spare scopes like the 80-volume *AN/PAS-13B* significantly restricts your extraction profits.
3. **Stamina Drain Mechanics:** Heavier weapon builds increase arm stamina depletion when aiming down sights for extended periods.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Active Matter Weapon Attachments

### What are the best Active Matter weapon attachments for controlling AK recoil?
For AK-pattern rifles like the AK-103 and AK-12, combining the **PT-3 Telescopic Stock** or **AK Tactical Stock** with a **PBS-1 Silencer** or **HX6 Silencer** offers the best recoil reduction. For optics, the **OKP-7** or **1P87** Collimator mounts directly to the Dovetail High Mount without requiring additional rail adapters.

### How do rail mount types affect attachment compatibility?
Attachments are strictly locked to matching mounting systems. Picatinny-mounted optics cannot attach to Soviet Dovetail side brackets without specialized conversion mounts, and standard AR buffer-tube stocks will not fit AK tang receivers. Always check the attachment interface listed in your weapon's inspection tab.

### Are thermal scopes worth the weight and volume penalty in Active Matter?
Thermal optics like the **Thermal Scope S350F** and **AN/PAS-13B** are invaluable during night operations, coastal fog conditions, and smoke grenade pushes. However, due to their substantial weight (up to 2.5 kg) and inventory volume (up to 80 units), they are best reserved for dedicated squad marksmen rather than front-line rushers.

### Do silencers decrease weapon damage in Active Matter?
Silencers in *Active Matter* do not reduce base bullet damage. However, certain heavy suppressors—such as the **BN45 Silencer**—apply a minor penalty to bullet velocity (-1.5%). This slightly increases bullet drop and travel time over long distances without lowering overall impact damage.
