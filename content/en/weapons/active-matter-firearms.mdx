## Maintenance, Durability, and Malfunction Management

Environmental anomalies, toxic miasma, and rapid sustained firing rapidly degrade Active Matter firearms. As weapon condition drops below specific thresholds, mechanical failures occur during combat, turning straightforward engagements into life-threatening emergencies.

```
Malfunction Types:
- Failure to Extract (FTE): Empty casing lodges in ejection port.
- Stovepipe Jam: Shell gets pinned vertically by closing bolt.
- Misfire: Primer fails to ignite due to fouled firing pin.
- Overheating: Barrel warp causing catastrophic bullet dispersion.
```

## Tactical Gunplay Tips for Quantum Firefights

Surviving intense firefights requires combining marksmanship with tactical positioning and spatial awareness.

- **Pre-fire Anomaly Corners**: Extradimensional monsters and ambushing players often emit distinct audio cues or visual particle distortions. Use point-fire hip shooting while slicing corners.
- **Stagger Your Magazines**: Load the top 10 rounds of your magazine with high-tier AP ammo to crack heavy body armor, backed by cost-effective ball rounds for fleshy targets.
- **Control Firing Cadence**: Sustained automatic fire increases barrel temperature exponentially, spiking your weapon jam probability and degrading accuracy. Fire in deliberate 3-to-5 round bursts.
- **Utilize Tactical Leaning**: Exposing only your weapon and helmet around hard cover minimizes your target profile against incoming high-caliber sniper rounds.
- **Listen for Weapon Clicks**: Auditory feedback in *Active Matter* is precise; hearing an opponent's dry-fire click or jam animation is your green light to push aggressively.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the most beginner-friendly Active Matter firearms for solo players?
The AK-74M and SKS are the premier choices for solo beginners. Both platforms utilize affordable, high-penetration ammunition (5.45mm and 7.62x39mm), require minimal modding to perform effectively, and boast high durability that resists anomaly degradation.

### How does ammo type impact armor penetration in Active Matter?
Ammo caliber and bullet composition dictate armor penetration. Armor-piercing rounds (such as 5.56 M995 or 7.62 M61) bypass high-tier armor plates to deal direct vital organ damage, whereas hollow-point or buckshot rounds deal devastating flesh damage against unarmored limbs.

### How does anomaly exposure affect Active Matter firearms over time?
Firing weapons inside active quantum rifts accelerates component wear by up to 200%. Exotic particles cause carbon fouling and receiver corrosion, significantly increasing the probability of mechanical stovepipes and misfires if weapons are not cleaned between raids.

### Can weapon attachments be salvaged or swapped during an active raid?
Yes. Operatives can strip non-vital attachments like tactical flashlights, red dot sights, and foregrips from downed enemies in-raid. However, major modifications like barrel replacements and gas block tuning must be completed at the hideout workbench.
