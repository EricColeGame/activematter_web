### Top Assault Rifles

#### M4A1
The M4A1 is widely considered the gold standard in the current meta. With a 750 RPM rate of fire and low base recoil (0.12), you can hold accurate sustained bursts against mobile targets. Its light 2.7 kg frame preserves your stamina pool for rapid repositioning, and the quick 2.67-second reload keeps downtime minimal during hectic firefights.

#### SCAR-L
For players who prefer pinpoint tap-firing over spray-and-pray tactics, the SCAR-L is a standout rifle. Its smooth 0.10 recoil index makes it the most stable 5.56mm platform in the game. While its 625 RPM is slightly slower than the M4A1, the improved shot placement and rapid 2.3-second reload make it exceptionally lethal in ranged duels.

#### AK-103
The AK-103 delivers heavy 7.62×39mm rounds with substantial armor penetration and high per-shot damage. It kicks harder with a 0.14 recoil value, but players who master burst-firing will melt armored enemies much faster than 5.56mm users can manage.

```
       M4A1 (High RPM / Agile) <---> SCAR-L (Low Recoil / Precision)
                                 ^
                                 |
                     AK-103 (Raw Stopping Power)
```

### Top Submachine Guns

#### MP5
The MP5 is an exceptionally reliable close-quarters weapon. Featuring a 0.07 recoil stat, bullet spread remains virtually flat even when strafing or hip-firing. While its 3.5-second reload demands safe cover, its lightweight mobility and common 9×19mm ammo make it an essential raid companion.

#### Scorpion EVO 3
Boasting an incredible 1080 RPM, the Scorpion EVO 3 has the fastest time-to-kill among all rapid-fire weapons. It tears through enemy health pools before opponents can react. It requires careful trigger discipline to avoid burning through magazines, but its 2.77 kg weight and 3.0-second reload make it unmatched for aggressive room clearing.

### Top Precision & Shotgun Options

#### SV-98
When you need to secure a perimeter or eliminate snipers across extraction zones, the SV-98 delivers exceptional consistency. Chambered in 7.62×51mm, it provides clean, one-shot kill potential. Its 2.2-second bolt-cycle reload allows for rapid follow-up shots on armored targets.

#### MB590
The MB590 is the premier pump-action shotgun for holding interior corners. Each shell delivers devastating pellet spread capable of one-shotting unarmored and lightly armored players. While the total tube reload takes 6.11 seconds, you can interrupt the animation to fire individual rounds in an emergency.

*   **Primary:** SV-98 (High-magnification optic).
*   **Secondary:** MP5 (Equipped for hipfire accuracy).

### Tactical Tips for Extraction Survival

*   **Manage Equipment Weight:** Weapons like the SV-98 (6.2 kg) and SPAS-12 (4.4 kg) add substantial weight. Keep your overall kit in the green weight threshold to preserve stamina for fast tactical retreats.
*   **Pace Your Bursts:** Automatic weapons like the AK-103 kick sharply after the first 4–5 rounds. Fire in controlled bursts to keep your crosshair locked onto the upper chest and head.
*   **Reload Early in Cover:** Shotguns such as the SPAS-12 (7.86s) and MB590 (6.11s) take a long time to fully reload. Top off your magazine tube after every skirmish rather than waiting until you run empty.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the absolute best weapon in the Active Matter weapon tier list?
The **M4A1** is widely considered the best all-around weapon on the **Active Matter weapon tier list**. Its balanced 750 RPM fire rate, low 0.12 recoil index, lightweight 2.7 kg profile, and fast 2.67-second reload make it adaptable for virtually any firefight.

### Which weapon is best for beginner solo players?
For solo beginners, the **MP5** and **M4A1** are top choices. Both firearms feature minimal recoil, predictable spray patterns, and use common ammunition types that you can easily loot during raids.

### Are shotguns viable on open extraction maps?
Yes, but they should be carried as secondary weapons. While shotguns like the **MB590** dominate tight rooms, stairwells, and extraction bunkers, they drop off steeply past 10 meters. Pair them with an assault rifle or DMR for open-field combat.

### How does weapon weight affect gunplay in Active Matter?
Weapon weight directly impacts your character's movement speed, sprint duration, and stamina recovery. Heavy guns like the **SV-98** (6.2 kg) slow your mobility, making lightweight sidearms and agile secondary SMGs critical for repositioning.
