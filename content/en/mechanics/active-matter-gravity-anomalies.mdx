## Types of Active Matter Gravity Anomalies and Environmental Hazards

Not every spatial rupture behaves the same way. While standard terrain hazards deal flat elemental or toxic damage, **Active Matter gravity anomalies** dynamically manipulate spatial mass and kinetic momentum. Identifying the anomaly type before breaching a room or crossing an open field is critical to maintaining combat readiness.

### 1. Singularity Wells (Vortex Traps)
Singularity Wells are the most lethal variant encountered in deep industrial zones. They pull all physical entities—players, throwables, and loose containers—toward an ultra-dense central vortex. Once caught beyond the event horizon threshold, escaping without tactical mobility gear is nearly impossible.

### 2. Repulsion Fields (Kinetic Deflectors)
Repulsion fields exert an explosive outward kinetic burst. While they rarely deal direct crushing damage, they violently propel operators into solid geometry, causing massive blunt force trauma and fall damage. 

### 3. Null-G Vortices (Zero-Gravity Pockets)
Suspended pockets of zero gravity remove traction and foot mobility. Operators caught within them float helplessly, becoming easy targets for enemy snipers while suffering from severe weapon recoil penalties.

### 4. Shear Pressure Zones (Compressive Strata)
These anomalies fluctuate in vertical waves. Stepping into a shear zone triples gravity for a split second before oscillating back, instantly crippling lower limbs and destroying movement speed.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Active Matter Gravity Anomalies

### How do I escape if caught in a Singularity Well?
If caught in the outer radius of a Singularity Well, immediately turn your operator 90 degrees away from the center to avoid fighting direct resistance. Sprint diagonally while executing short, rhythmic jumps to exploit kinetic vector falloff. If you have a thruster pack or inertial anchor, deploy it immediately before your stamina hits zero.

### Do Active Matter gravity anomalies spawn high-tier loot?
Yes. The highest concentrations of pure Active Matter compounds and unstable crystalline artifacts form along the event horizons of severe gravitational anomalies. Harvesting these nodes requires approaching with containment vessels while managing field exposure times.

### Can bullets penetrate or curve through Active Matter gravity anomalies?
Yes. Projectiles entering a gravitational field experience significant trajectory deviation depending on bullet caliber, velocity, and distance from the core. High-velocity sniper rounds will bend along the gravitational curve, while low-velocity subsonic rounds can be completely pulled into the singularity core.

### Do gravity anomalies change locations during a match?
While most anomalies remain anchored to specific spatial coordinates, end-game extraction collapses can trigger "wandering anomalies." These mobile distortions drift across extraction zones, forcing squads to adapt their defensive perimeters during the final extraction countdown.
