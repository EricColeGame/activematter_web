## Understanding the Raid Loop: Quantum Zones and Multiverse Anomalies

The core gameplay loop revolves around high-stakes PvPvE excursions into locations torn from standard spacetime. In these zones, players fight not only hostile automated defense systems and anomalous horrors but also rival operatives pursuing the exact same prize: Active Matter. 

Active Matter is an exotic, reality-altering resource essential for crafting elite weaponry, expanding your base storage, and fabricating tactical equipment. Securing raw Active Matter requires locating concentrated nodes, harvesting them under fire, and successfully carrying them through active extraction points before the local zone destabilizes.

```
[Infil / Spawn] ➔ [Drone Recon & Node Identification] ➔ [PvPvE Combat & Harvesting] ➔ [Exfil Point Activation] ➔ [Secure Extraction]
```

Raids feature two primary avenues for acquiring wealth:
* **Passive Matter Harvesting:** Extracting ambient matter from environmental reservoirs spread across industrial sectors.
* **Active Node Battles:** Contesting dense, high-tier anomalies that emit periodic energy spikes, attracting every hostile squad on the map.

## Step-by-Step Survival and Extraction Strategy

Navigating a raid requires a strict timeline. Staying too long increases the risk of encountering elite AI and player ambushes, while rushing too quickly leaves you under-resourced.

```
Raid Timeline:
0:00 - 5:00   ➔ Spawn In, Deploy Harpy Drone, Check Extraction Timers
5:00 - 15:00  ➔ Clear AI, Loot Secondary Objectives, Secure Safe Container Loot
15:00 - 25:00 ➔ Contest Active Matter Node, Engage/Evade Rival Squads
25:00 - Exfil ➔ Pop Smoke/Beacon, Clear Exfil Campers, Extract Safely
```

As outlined in this **Active Matter extraction guide**, timing your movement according to the raid's three distinct phases dramatically increases your survival rate:

### Phase 1: Infiltration and Reconnaissance (First 5 Minutes)
Immediately upon spawning, move to hard cover and listen for nearby weapon signatures. Launch a Scout Drone "Harpy" to inspect adjacent buildings. Do not fire your weapon unless necessary—unsuppressed gunshots invite third-party attacks from neighboring spawns.

### Phase 2: Active Harvesting and Safe Storage (5 to 20 Minutes)
Push toward industrial storage rooms and scientific research nodes. When collecting Active Matter Crystals or rare scope attachments, transfer them into your safe container immediately. If engaging enemy squads, use grenades (such as F-1 or Incendiary) to flush defenders out of narrow bunker corridors.

### Phase 3: Secure Exfiltration (Final 10 Minutes)
Extraction zones are prime ambush locations. Approach exfiltration points with extreme caution:
* Deploy your drone to sweep nearby bushes, elevated crane towers, and rooftops.
* Throw smoke grenades across open extraction pads to obscure sniper sightlines.
* Place a Respawn Beacon in a hidden structure 100 meters away from the extraction zone so fallen teammates can re-enter the fight.

| Raid Phase | Primary Objective | High-Risk Mistake to Avoid | Optimal Gear Behavior |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Early Raid** | Identify exfils & drone sweep | Sprinting across open fields | Keep weapon at high ready; stay in cover |
| **Mid Raid** | Harvest Active Matter nodes | Greed looting without watching flank | Store crystals in safe container; cycle plates |
| **Late Raid** | Trigger extraction beacon | Rushing directly onto the exfil pad | Clear perimeter with thermal optics; use smoke |

## Advanced Combat Tactics in PvPvE Engagements

Surviving combat against both mutated entities and skilled human operators requires sound tactical fundamentals. To push your survival rate higher, our **Active Matter extraction guide** recommends mastering these core combat behaviors:

### Thermal Sight Dominance
Navigating foggy or dim Soviet laboratories gives a distinct advantage to players equipped with thermal optics, such as the Thermal Scope S350F 2.0x. Use thermal scopes to identify player silhouettes hiding behind foliage or deep inside darkened window frames.

### Health and Fracture Management
Standard first aid kits restore raw hitpoints, but they do not fix bone fractures or severe blood loss caused by heavy caliber rounds.
* Always bind **Streaming Injectors** and **Painkillers** to quick-use slots.
* Painkillers allow your operative to sprint even with broken leg bones, letting you reach an extraction gate under fire.

Based on player experience, squads that assign dedicated roles—one point-man with a shotgun/SMG and heavy Titan plates, one mid-range rifleman with an M4A1 or SCAR-L, and one marksman with an SV-98 or M110A1—consistently out-position uncoordinated groups.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the most critical item to secure in an Active Matter extraction guide?
Your top priority should always be Active Matter Crystals and high-tier crafting blueprints, placed directly into your safe container. These resources are required to upgrade your stash, expand your replicator queue, and fabricate top-tier survival gear.

### How do Respawn Beacons function during a raid?
When deployed by a squad member, a Respawn Beacon serves as a quantum anchor. If a teammate dies, they can spend available raid tokens to materialize at the beacon's location, retaining the ability to support the team and extract gear.

### Can solo players extract consistently against three-man squads?
Yes. Solo operatives should rely heavily on stealth, the Scout Drone "Harpy", and silenced mid-range weapons like the SOK-94 or M4A1. Avoid contesting loud central nodes directly; instead, pick off wounded players retreating toward extraction points or harvest secondary nodes along the map periphery.
