## Understanding Loot Tiers and Item Value in Active Matter

In *Active Matter*, looting is not merely about grabbing every piece of scrap you find. Because your carrying capacity and stamina are strictly tied to weight and physical grid space, understanding **value-per-slot** is the single most important skill for long-term progression.

Items fall into five distinct categories: Pure Active Matter (the core currency/resource used for anomalous crafting and black-market trade), Military Hardware, Research Tech, Medical Supplies, and Basic Barter Junk. 

```
Pure Active Matter & Cores > High-End Weapon Mods > Keycards & Encrypted Data > Military Armor > Raw Scrap
```

Community reports indicate that many beginners make the mistake of hauling bulky low-tier armor plates or common assault rifles that take up 8 to 12 slots while selling for negligible payouts. Instead, your primary objective should be compact, high-density valuables such as Quantum Condensate, micro-processors, and specialized optical attachments.

## High-Value Loot Zones and Anomaly Hotspots

High-tier resources spawn dynamically around anomaly clusters and fortified research compounds. While exploring the game world via the [official Active Matter portal](https://activematter.game/), players quickly discover that the most dangerous zones yield the greatest financial returns.

When planning your raid routes, you must weigh the threat level of rival operators and AI guards against the potential loot density. Hot zones feature reinforced security boxes, laboratory servers, and unstable matter nodes that produce quantum resources.

Following this **Active Matter loot guide** route planning ensures you avoid wandering aimlessly. For maximum efficiency, target one primary POI (such as the Central Sub-Collider) during the first 5 minutes of a raid, clear the secure containers, and rotate toward a secondary low-risk zone before heading toward the extraction beacon.

## Optimal Inventory Management and Slot Prioritization

Inventory tetris can cost you your life in high-stakes extraction shooters. A fundamental rule in any **Active Matter loot guide** is slot efficiency: never spend more than 15 seconds sorting your backpack inside an unsecure room.

Equip tactical rigs and backpacks with favorable slot-to-space ratios. If you pick up enemy weapons, strip high-tier muzzle devices, magnified scopes, and tactical lasers, then leave the heavy base receiver on the ground unless you have empty primary weapon slots.

### Backpack & Rig Capacity Comparison

| Storage Equipment | Grid Space Provided | Internal Layout Breakdown | Weight Penalty | Best Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Scout Rig Mk. I** | 10 Slots | 2x2, 1x2 (x3) | Minimal (-2% Speed) | Low-cost scrap runs & speed farming |
| **Tactical Assault Rig IV** | 18 Slots | 2x2 (x2), 1x2 (x4), 1x1 (x2) | Light (-4% Speed) | Balanced PvPvE raiding |
| **Hazardous Field Pack (30L)** | 24 Slots | 4x6 Open Grid | Medium (-6% Speed) | High-value tech and hardware hauling |
| **Expedition Anomaly Rucksack** | 36 Slots | 6x6 Open Grid (Matter Shielded) | Heavy (-10% Speed) | Squad-backed deep laboratory raids |

### On-the-Fly Loot Triage Checklist
* **Always secure 1x1 items first:** Data drives, keys, and concentrated matter provide maximum profit per square inch.
* **Strip enemy guns:** Remove red dots, thermal sights, suppressors, and extended magazines before discarding bulky frames.
* **Consolidate ammo:** Empty half-spent magazines into your secure container if they hold armor-piercing ammunition.
* **Drop barter junk for medical supplies:** If your health or limbs take damage, survival takes precedence over industrial wire bundles.

Accumulating millions of credits worth of gear means nothing if you fail to extract. As the match timer winds down, extraction points become prime ambush locations for campers and roaming squads.

This **Active Matter loot guide** emphasizes surviving over greed. When your backpack is 80% full of high-tier items, disengage from unnecessary firefights and begin your rotation toward the nearest extraction point.

## Active Matter Looting: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the most valuable item to farm according to this Active Matter loot guide?
The most valuable items per slot are **Singularity Cores**, **Pure Active Matter canisters**, and **Encrypted Laboratory Access Cards**. These items take up minimal inventory space (1x1 or 1x2) and yield massive returns on the market or in anomalous crafting recipes.

### Should I carry weapons out of the raid or dismantle them?
Unless you are missing a primary weapon in your secondary slot, it is generally better to strip high-value attachments (thermal sights, silencers, advanced grips) and leave the bulky rifle frame behind. Attachments offer a significantly higher value-per-slot ratio than stripped weapon bodies.

### How do conditional matter extraction points work?
Conditional extractions allow players to bypass crowded standard extraction points by spending a set amount of harvested Active Matter at an anomalous beacon. This ensures a quick, quiet exit, making it the safest choice when carrying an inventory full of high-tier loot.

### Where can I find keycards for locked bunker doors?
Keycards spawn inside executive office desks, lab server racks, and occasionally in the pockets of elite AI commanders located in high-risk zones like the Central Sub-Collider Complex. Always refer back to our **Active Matter loot guide** for updated node paths and room prioritization strategies.
