## Edition Comparison: Choosing the Right Digital Tier

Selecting the appropriate package before initiating your **Active Matter download** determines your starting liquid capital, stash volume, weapons, and crafting capabilities. Every tier provides access to the full game, but higher packages significantly reduce early-game economic friction through expanded storage and exclusive replicator blueprints.

### Standard Edition vs. Advanced Edition

The **Standard Edition** serves as the baseline entry point. It supplies 25,000 Credits, 300 Active Matter Crystals, and a reliable arsenal of practical weapons like the SOK-94 Vepr Carbine, MB590 shotgun, MP5 submachine guns, and M9 sidearms. It also packs essential survival tools, including small safe containers and respawn beacons.

The **Advanced Edition** steps up your operational capacity with 60,000 Credits, 1,300 Crystals, your first storage slot expansion, and an expanded replicator queue. You also unlock the permanent recipe for the **Scout Drone "Harpy"** and the personal backup weapon: brass knuckles knife "Bouncer."

### Premium Edition vs. Elite Squad Edition

The **Premium Edition** provides extensive survival utility. Boasting 135,000 Credits, 3,800 Crystals, three full storage expansions, and two replicator queue expansions, it offers high tier support right out of the gate. Players receive the exclusive **Shell "Reaper"** character, high-tier weaponry (PKM machine gun, M110A1 marksman rifle, SCAR-L), thermal optics, and heavy crafting blueprints like the **Assault Quad Bike "Bonecrusher"** and the **Robot Dog "胡安一号"**.

The **Elite Squad Edition** contains all Premium perks while including **two additional Standard Edition digital keys**, making it the best option for organized three-player raid teams looking to download together.

## Surviving Quantum Raids: Early-Game Field Guide

Once you finish your **Active Matter download** and deploy into your first anomalous zone, normal tactical shooter rules no longer fully apply. You are not just fighting enemy players—you are combating reality distortions, spatial fractures, and unstable matter sources.

```
Deploy Zone ➔ Locate Passive Source ➔ Harvest Matter ➔ Deploy Beacon ➔ Extract
```

### Essential Beginner Tactics

1. **Protect Your Matter Crystals:** Never carry active matter crystals in external backpack slots. Secure them immediately inside your Safe Container so they persist through an operator casualty.
2. **Respect the Multiverse Anomalies:** Spacetime shifts alter map pathways in real time. If a corridor or Soviet warehouse begins showing visual distortion artifacts, check your environmental scanner for gravity traps or sudden teleportation rifts.
3. **Control Passive Sources Early:** Map sectors containing passive active matter wells attract rival squads quickly. Set up crossfires using the Scout Drone "Harpy" before moving into the open to harvest.
4. **Deploy Respawn Beacons Strategically:** Position beacons behind cover near external extract zones rather than inside hot contested objectives to avoid enemy camp ambushes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I start an Active Matter download without purchasing an edition?
No. An **Active Matter download** requires an authorized game license tied to your account. If you attempt to access the game without purchasing a digital edition, the store system will prevent file delivery until a transaction is verified.

### Can I upgrade my edition after completing my initial Active Matter download?
Yes. According to player experience and standard store functionality, players can upgrade from the Standard Edition to Advanced or Premium by paying the price difference through the official store portal, unlocking additional stash slots and blueprints instantly.

### Are Replicator recipes lost when an operator dies during a raid?
No. Permanent Replicator recipes (such as the Scout Drone, Bonecrusher Quad, or Robot Dog) remain unlocked in your hideout forever. Only the physical synthesized units brought into a raid can be looted by enemies if lost.

### What is the advantage of the Elite Squad Edition over Premium?
The Elite Squad Edition includes the complete Premium Edition content package for the primary account, plus two separate Standard Edition access keys. It is designed specifically for three-player teams who want to coordinate their **Active Matter download** and jump into raids together at a reduced bundle cost.
