### Graphics Card (GPU) Demands & Resolution Targets

The graphics pipeline in *Active Matter* handles real-time visual distortion effects, dark atmospheric lighting composed alongside iconic horror soundscapes, and high-fidelity weapon textures. While entry-level cards like the GTX 1050 Ti and RX 470 can launch the title at 1080p with low presets, mid-range GPUs are necessary for stable competitive frame rates.

| GPU Class / Model | Target Resolution | Preset Level | Expected Framerate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **GTX 1050 Ti / RX 470** | 1080p | Low Preset (FSR / XeSS Quality) | 35–45 FPS |
| **Intel ARC A580 / GTX 1660 Super** | 1080p | Medium Preset | 50–60 FPS |
| **RTX 2060 / RX 5700 XT / ARC A770** | 1080p / 1440p | High Preset | 65–85 FPS |
| **RTX 3070 / RX 6700 XT** | 1440p | Ultra Preset | 90–115 FPS |
| **RTX 4070 Ti / RX 7900 XT or better** | 4K (2160p) | Ultra Preset | 100+ FPS |

### Processor (CPU) and Multithreading Impact

Because *Active Matter* simulates spatial anomalies, bullet drop, active AI monsters, and player tracking simultaneously, quad-core processors like the i5-6600 represent the absolute floor. A 6-core / 12-thread CPU (such as the Ryzen 5 3600 or Core i5-8700) is strongly advised to prevent micro-stuttering during multi-squad firefights in sprawling industrial research facilities.

### Memory (RAM) Overhead

While 8 GB is the listed minimum, player experience in modern multiplayer extraction shooters indicates that 8 GB systems frequently experience hitching when Windows background tasks run concurrently. Upgrading to 16 GB in a dual-channel configuration (2x8 GB) delivers far smoother frame pacing and eliminates memory thrashing.

### The Non-Negotiable SSD Requirement

Unlike older tactical titles, an SSD is explicitly marked as mandatory in the **Active Matter system requirements**. Quantum unstable zones dynamically load assets and atmospheric distortions as you breach new zones. Installing the game on a traditional mechanical Hard Disk Drive (HDD) will result in severe texture pop-in, prolonged load times, and potential extraction desynchronization.

### Component Synergy Tips
*   **Dual-Channel Memory:** Running two RAM sticks instead of one increases memory bandwidth by up to 15–20% in dynamic shooter environments.
*   **Storage Bus:** A standard SATA SSD will fulfill the minimum requirement, but an M.2 NVMe SSD operating over PCIe Gen 3 or Gen 4 drastically accelerates initial server loading and respawn loops.
*   **Power Supply Headroom:** Ensure your power supply unit (PSU) maintains at least 100W–150W of headroom above your total system wattage to prevent sudden shutoffs during peak GPU clock boosts.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Active Matter System Requirements

### Can my PC run Active Matter on a traditional Hard Disk Drive (HDD)?
No. Matter Team has specified that an SSD is mandatory in the official **Active Matter system requirements**. Installing the game on a standard mechanical HDD will cause major asset streaming lag, texture loading delays, and potential disconnects in quantum anomaly zones.

### What are the minimum Active Matter system requirements for 1080p 60 FPS gameplay?
To achieve a reliable 60 FPS at 1080p resolution, your rig should match or slightly exceed the recommended hardware: an Intel Core i5-8700 or AMD Ryzen 5 3600 processor, 16 GB of dual-channel RAM, and an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060, AMD Radeon RX 5700 XT, or Intel ARC A770 graphics card.

### Is Active Matter compatible with Steam Deck and Linux at launch?
*Active Matter* lists official support for 64-bit Linux distributions (including Ubuntu 26.04). While native Linux is supported, official Steam Deck verification remains classified as "Unknown" pending launch-day compatibility with its BattlEye kernel anti-cheat configuration.

### How much storage space is required to install Active Matter?
The game requires at least **45 GB of free SSD space**. It is advisable to maintain an extra 15–20 GB of buffer space on your drive for future content patches, map expansions, and shader caches.
