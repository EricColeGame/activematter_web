## Core Gameplay: PvPvE Extraction in a Fractured Multiverse

At its foundation, *Active Matter* combines the tension of high-risk extraction shooters with supernatural sci-fi horror. You play as an operative bound to an endless time loop. Each raid forces you to navigate quantum unstable zones where space and gravity collapse without warning.

```
[Infiltrate Unstable Zone] ➔ [Harvest Active Matter & Military Loot] ➔ [Survive SCP Anomalies & Rivals] ➔ [Extract Before Timeline Collapse] ➔ [Expand Syndicate Base & Craft Gear]
```

### 1. Quantum Extraction Raids
Players can deploy solo or assemble small tactical fireteams. Your primary goal is to collect Active Matter—the multiversal resource capable of altering spacetime. You can obtain Active Matter through several methods:
* Siphoning directly from raw environmental Active Matter clusters.
* Eliminating and harvesting mutated survivors and horrific AM-transformed creatures.
* Ambushing rival operatives and looting their secured containers before they reach an extraction point.

### 2. Temporal Collapse Mechanics
Unlike standard extraction maps with static extraction timers, zones in *Active Matter* suffer from accelerating quantum degradation. If you linger too long hunting for rare weapon parts, the entire pocket reality will destabilize, vaporizing your operative along with all unextracted inventory.

### 3. Syndicate Bases and Multiverse Item Replication
Between deployments, operatives return to their syndicate headquarters. By investing gathered Active Matter, you can replicate authentic, real-world weapons and tactical gear pulled from alternate timelines. Syndicates also clash in large-scale dedicated PvP battles over permanent, passive harvesting nodes that influence the wider multiverse economy.

### When will Active Matter unlock on Steam?
*Active Matter* is slated to unlock on Steam on **September 15, 2026**. Players can add the game to their Steam wishlist immediately to download day-one updates and access servers as soon as they go live.

### What are the minimum specs to run Active Matter on PC?
To run the game, your PC will need at least an Intel Core i5-6600 or AMD Ryzen 3 1300X processor, 8 GB of RAM, a GTX 1050 Ti or RX 470 graphics card, and 45 GB of free space on a dedicated solid-state drive (SSD).

### Does Active Matter work on Linux and SteamOS?
Yes, the developers have provided official minimum and recommended system requirements for modern 64-bit Linux distributions and Ubuntu 26.04. However, because the game utilizes BattlEye kernel-level anti-cheat, full compatibility on handheld configurations such as the Steam Deck remains under ongoing evaluation by community reports and developer testing.
